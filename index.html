The Art of Layering

True Dum cooking is an architectural marvel. We alternate beds of 2-year aged basmati rice infused with whole cinnamon, bay leaves, and cardamom with tender cuts steeped in spiced gravy.

Gilded with golden caramelized onions (birista), crushed mint, and a golden thread of saffron milk, the vessel is sealed with fresh whole wheat dough to lock in every volatile aroma.

OUR HERITAGE

Forged in 900°F Clay Embers

At Tandoor Bar, we honor the ancient North Indian hearth. Our tandoors are hand-thrown from dense alluvial clay and seasoned with spinach and mustard paste before firing with aged charcoal.

No shortcuts, no artificial food colorings. Just pure heritage spice blends toasted daily, slow charcoal smokiness, and the patience of generational craft.

Saffron from the Pampore Valleys of Kashmir
