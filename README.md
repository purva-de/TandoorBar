# Noodle Bar (2004) - Restaurants Chain Website

A recreation of the **"Restaurants Chain Website"** design concept by **Mirhayot** for **Eloqwnt** on [Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/shots/26283853-Restaurants-Chain-Website).

---

## 🍜 Key Highlights & Features

1. **Pixel-Accurate Hero Section**:
   - Iconic high-contrast editorial typography: `NOODLE BAR (2004)`.
   - Floating interactive 3D cursor icon with smooth hover micro-interaction.
   - 3-column metadata bar: `Services` (RESTAURANT), `Location` (EAST VILLAGE → NY, 171 1st Avenue), and `Founder` (DAVID CHANG).
   - Warm-toned hero showcase card with the original glazed wings presentation and interactive dish spotlight carousel.

2. **Restaurants Chain Multi-Location Grid**:
   - Showcases the expansion from the original 2004 East Village counter to modern locations in Columbus Circle (NYC), Las Vegas (The Strip), and Downtown Los Angeles (Arts District).
   - Operating hours, dining atmosphere, and direct action triggers for booking and pickup.

3. **Curated Seasonal Menu with Interactive Category Filters**:
   - Filterable categories: *All Offerings*, *Buns & Starters*, *Ramen & Noodles*, *Large Format Dining*, and *Sake & Cocktails*.
   - High-resolution food photography, pricing, dietary indicators, and "Add to Bag" functionality.

4. **Heritage & Founder Story ("David Chang - Since 2004")**:
   - Deep dive into how a small 27-seat noodle shop on 1st Avenue changed the American dining landscape.
   - Atmospheric open kitchen counter photography with timeline milestones.

5. **Interactive Table Reservation System**:
   - Real-time location selector, date picker, meal timing slots (Lunch & Dinner), party size selection, and seating area preference (Counter, Dining Room, Chef's Table).
   - Generates an instant booking confirmation ticket with unique reservation ID.

6. **Interactive Ordering Bag / Offcanvas Drawer**:
   - Live badge counter, quantity increment/decrement, real-time NYC sales tax calculation, and smooth checkout confirmation.

7. **Theme Switcher (Light / Dark Mode)**:
   - Full CSS variable architecture enabling seamless transitions between editorial light mode and sleek dark mode.

---

## 🎨 Design System & Accessibility Compliance

- **Semantic HTML5**: Full structural compliance with accessible `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<aside>`, and `<footer>` elements.
- **8px Grid System**: Consistent spacing rhythm (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `40px`, `48px`, `64px`, `80px`, `96px`, `128px`).
- **Reduced Motion**: Full `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` support.
- **Typography**: Google Fonts `Playfair Display` (editorial serif) and `Plus Jakarta Sans` (modern sans-serif).
